The problem

The first record of care is written late, from memory. Everything after depends on it.

11 pm, Avinashi Road, Coimbatore. A bike crash. The EMT is the only clinician on board: one hand on a head wound, the other on an IV line. Nothing is written until after the handover. The ER's next dose, the audit, the insurance claim and the medico-legal case all start from that record.

How it works

Six steps. All on the phone.

The EMT says “gave 4 mg ondansetron IV”. Thunai picks out drug, dose, route and time, reads it back for a quick “yes”, and adds it to the record. No cloud in the loop.

  1. 01

    Speak or type

    Push-to-talk or a wake word, so gloved hands stay on the patient. Typing works too.

  2. 02

    Transcribe on the phone

    Quantised Whisper-small via whisper.cpp, tuned on Indian-accented English with siren noise mixed in.

  3. 03

    Pick out the facts

    Clinical NER tags drug, dose, unit, route, time and procedure.

  4. 04

    Clean up and check

    “Emeset” becomes ondansetron, “5 min back” becomes 23:02. Dose checked against protocol, then read back.

  5. 05

    Build the record

    FHIR-lite entries (MedicationAdministration, Procedure, Observation), each hash-chained to the last.

  6. 06

    Hand over and sync

    One-page ATMIST summary and a QR code at the ER door. Syncs to the hospital system when the signal returns.

Why crews can trust it

If it isn’t sure, it asks. If it isn’t said, it isn’t written.

The AI only hears and labels. Checks, the record and the handover summary are rules and templates, so a drug that was never given can’t appear.

Works with zero network

Speech, NER and the record all run on a mid-range Android phone. No dead zones, no per-minute speech bill.

Speaks like Indian EMTs

Indian-accented English, brand names like Emeset, Avil and Deriphyllin, “1 amp”, “5 minutes back”.

Never guesses quietly

Every drug and dose is read back. “Fifteen” vs “fifty” and out-of-range doses get flagged.

Audit-ready from the first word

Timestamped, hash-chained entries, so tampering shows. Corrections are new entries.
